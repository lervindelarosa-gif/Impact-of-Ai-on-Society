### 🚀 What We Covered in Our Presentation:
* **Project Overview:** Analyzing what drives audience engagement (likes and comments) on trending YouTube videos in Germany.
* **Data Sources:** Combining a 2026 live API snapshot with a massive historical Kaggle dataset (~55,000 videos from 2020–2024).
* **Key Findings & Myth Busting:** 
  * Why longer videos (20–60 minutes) actually outperform Shorts on engagement.
  * How categories with the most views differ from the ones with the highest interaction rates.
  * Discovering that clickbait titles (`!`, `?`, or CAPS) actually result in **44% fewer median views** rather than more!
* **Team Reflection & Next Steps:** Handling data biases, cleaning challenges, and ideas for future analysis.
